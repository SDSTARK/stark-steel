Low Carbon Bright Alkaline Steel Wire Description

Low carbon bright alkaline steel wire is manufactured from quality low-carbon steel rod by cleaning, drawing and bright annealing or alkaline surface treatment. The result is a smooth, bright wire free from mill scale and oil, with predictable mechanical properties and outstanding ductility.

Thanks to its clean surface, this wire is the preferred substrate for hot-dip galvanizing, electro-galvanizing, PVC coating and plating. It feeds smoothly into automatic nail machines, mesh welders, staple lines and binding-wire equipment, reducing downtime and improving finished-product consistency.

We supply bright wire in a wide range of diameters and coil formats, from small spools for retail hardware to large coils for industrial wire-drawing plants. Custom tensile ranges, annealing conditions and packaging can be arranged to match your production requirements.

Key Features

  • Bright, clean surface: Alkaline cleaning removes scale and oil, leaving a smooth finish ideal for coating.
  • Excellent ductility: Low carbon content allows tight bending, twisting and forming without cracking.
  • Consistent tensile strength: Uniform hardness along the full length of every coil for reliable machine feeding.
  • Coating-friendly: Clean surface promotes strong adhesion for zinc, plastic and other coatings.
  • Flexible supply: Spools, coils or bobbins from 0.5 mm to 6.5 mm in diameters and custom weights.

Applications

Bright alkaline steel wire is widely used for binding wire, galvanized wire substrate, nails, staples, welded wire mesh, woven wire mesh, barbed wire, cable armoring, agricultural ties, horticultural supports, packaging and bundling. It is a core raw material for construction, agriculture, fencing and general hardware manufacturing.
