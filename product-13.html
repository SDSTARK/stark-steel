Frame Scaffolding System Description

Frame scaffolding — also known as door-frame or H-frame scaffolding — is one of the most popular and economical scaffolding systems for building construction and maintenance. The system is built from prefabricated welded steel frames that are quickly connected with cross braces and platform boards, creating a stable working platform at any required height. The standard unit shown in our product photos measures approximately 1800 mm (length) x 950 mm (width) x 1700 mm (height), and can be stacked vertically and combined with casters to form mobile access towers.

Our complete system includes all the components needed for safe erection: double-wide frames, cross bars, diagonal braces, adjustable feet, 8" casters, external supports, guardrail frames, skirting boards, platform plates and ladder racks. A typical mobile tower configuration can reach a total working height of 9.2 m (as illustrated in our engineering drawing), making it suitable for facade painting, plastering, installation, repair and routine building maintenance.

At Shandong Stark Steel, every frame is manufactured from high-grade structural steel and finished with a hot-dip galvanized coating, giving excellent corrosion resistance and a service life of 10+ years even in outdoor construction environments. Other types of modular scaffolding are also available on request.

Key Features

  • Fast assembly: Prefabricated frames and pin-lock cross braces allow a tower to be erected in minutes without special tools.
  • Mobile option: 8" swivel casters with brakes enable the whole tower to be moved safely between work areas.
  • Stable and safe: Includes external supports/outriggers, guardrail frames and skirting boards for compliance with EN 12810/12811 and OSHA.
  • Galvanized durability: Hot-dip galvanized surface resists rust and weather damage, reducing maintenance cost and extending service life.
  • Flexible height: Frames can be stacked and combined with ladder racks to reach working heights from 2 m up to 12 m.
  • Complete kit: One-stop supply of frames, braces, platforms, casters, jacks, guardrails and all accessories.

Applications

Frame scaffolding is widely used in residential and commercial building construction, interior and exterior wall painting, plastering and finishing, window and curtain-wall installation, HVAC and electrical maintenance, facade renovation, warehouse racking access, as well as temporary event staging and signage. Its light weight and fast erection make it especially popular for contractors who need to move frequently between work locations.
