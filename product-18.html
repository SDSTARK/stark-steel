Galvanized Color Coated Coil Description

Our galvanized color coated coil is produced by applying one or more layers of organic paint onto a hot-dip galvanized steel substrate, then baking to form a durable decorative and protective finish. The galvanized layer provides sacrificial corrosion protection, while the painted top coat offers additional weather, UV and chemical resistance, making it suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications.

Available thicknesses range from 0.12 mm to 2.0 mm, widths from 600 mm to 1250 mm, and zinc coatings from 30 to 275 g/m². We supply standard RAL colors and can match custom colors or provide special finishes such as matte, glossy, wrinkled, wood grain or camouflage patterns. Paint systems include economical PE, mid-durability SMP, high-durability HDP and premium PVDF for harsh environments.

Each coil is inspected for coating thickness, adhesion, color difference, gloss, bendability, zinc coating weight and mechanical properties before shipment. We can provide slitting, sheeting, profiling and other processing services according to project requirements, and deliver with seaworthy packaging to ensure the coils arrive in perfect condition.

Key Features

  • Hot-dip galvanized substrate with zinc coating 30-275 g/m² for excellent corrosion resistance.
  • Decorative and protective organic paint layers in RAL colors and custom shades.
  • Thickness 0.12-2.0 mm, width 600-1250 mm, coil ID 508/610 mm.
  • Paint options: PE, SMP, HDP, PVDF top coat; epoxy or polyester back coat.
  • Good formability for bending, roll forming, stamping and profiling.
  • Available with chromate passivation, oiling, anti-fingerprint or PVC film protection.

Applications

Galvanized color coated coil is widely used for roofing and walling sheets, corrugated profile panels, sandwich panel facing, garage doors, roller shutters, household appliances, kitchen cabinets, furniture, ceiling systems, interior decoration, sign boards, electrical enclosures, agricultural buildings, warehouses, prefabricated houses and industrial cladding where both aesthetics and durability are required.
