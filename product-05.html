Key Features

  • Excellent corrosion resistance: Chromium content forms a protective oxide layer that resists rust and chemicals.
  • High temperature resistance: 304 grade performs well up to about 800°C, suitable for heat exchange applications.
  • Hygienic and easy to clean: Smooth surface makes it ideal for food, beverage and pharmaceutical industries.
  • Good mechanical strength: High tensile strength with excellent toughness at low temperatures.
  • Complete grade range: 201, 304, 304L, 316, 316L, 321, 310S and more available.

Applications

Stainless steel pipes are widely used in fluid transportation (water, oil, gas), chemical processing, food & beverage lines, pharmaceutical plants, heat exchangers, architectural decoration, handrails and structural applications.
