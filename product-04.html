Steel Rebar (HRB400 / HRB500 / HRB600) Description

Our steel rebar is available in three primary grades: HRB400/HRB400E (400 MPa yield, standard for general construction), HRB500/HRB500E (500 MPa yield, widely used in mid-to-high-rise buildings and infrastructure), and HRB600 (600 MPa yield, ultra-high strength for mega projects). The "E" suffix denotes improved ductility for seismic-resistant design, with guaranteed strength-to-yield ratio and higher elongation.

Compared with conventional HRB400 rebar, HRB600 allows engineers to reduce steel tonnage by approximately 20-30% while maintaining the same load-bearing capacity, which also reduces transport, handling and placement costs. The bar surface is rolled with a well-defined pattern of longitudinal and transverse ribs to create strong mechanical bonding with concrete. Strict control of carbon equivalent ensures good weldability and bendability across all grades.

Bars are produced in straight lengths and can also be supplied in coils for automatic rebar processing machines. Each bundle is tagged with heat number, grade, diameter and length, and is supplied with a mill test certificate showing full chemical and mechanical results.

Key Features

  • Multi-grade supply: HRB400, HRB500 and HRB600 grades cover all project requirements from residential to mega infrastructure.
  • Strong concrete bond: Optimized rib pattern provides excellent pull-out resistance.
  • Good ductility: Minimum 14% elongation meets ductile design and seismic requirements.
  • Weldable and bendable: Controlled chemistry allows reliable site welding and bending.
  • Full traceability: Bundles tagged with heat number, grade and mechanical test results.

Applications

HRB600 rebar is used in high-rise buildings, long-span bridges, tunnels, dams, highway and railway projects, metro systems, power plants, industrial facilities and precast concrete components. It is the material of choice where high strength, reduced weight and reliable performance are critical.
