HRB600 High Strength Hot Rolled Reinforced Bar Description

HRB600 is a high-strength steel bar with a standard yield strength of 600MPa, hence it is also known as 600MPa grade high-strength steel bar. This type of steel reinforcement has been widely used in modern buildings and engineering structures, especially in situations where there is a need to improve structural performance and save materials.

Scope of Application

Widely used in houses, bridges, roads, especially railways and other civil engineering.

Key Features

  • Ultra-high strength: Standard yield strength of 600 MPa reduces steel usage by about 20% compared with HRB400, saving cost and weight.
  • Excellent seismic performance: Superior ductility and elongation meet the requirements of high-seismic zones.
  • Space saving: Higher strength allows smaller bar diameters and lighter structures, increasing usable floor area.
  • Good weldability: Suitable for flash butt welding and mechanical coupler connections.
  • Stable quality: Produced with controlled rolling and cooling process, with complete mill certificates.

Applications

HRB600 rebar is mainly used in high-rise buildings, large-span bridges, nuclear power plants, dams, underground engineering and other key infrastructure where high strength and seismic resistance are critical.
