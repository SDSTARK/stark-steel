Galvanized steel sheet, also known as galvanized steel plate, refers to a type of steel that has been coated with a thin layer of zinc to protect it from corrosion. The galvanization process is usually done through galvanizing, where the steel is immersed in molten zinc, or through electro-galvanizing, where zinc is applied using an electric current.

The zinc coating serves as a protective barrier that prevents moisture and oxygen from reaching the steel surface, which helps to avoid rusting and extends the lifespan of the steel. Galvanized steel is widely used in various industries, including construction, automotive, and manufacturing, for products such as roofing, siding, ducts, and steel structures.

Key advantages of galvanized steel include:

Corrosion Resistance: The zinc coating offers excellent protection against rust and corrosion, even in harsh environments.

Key Features

  • Effective anti-corrosion: Zinc coating protects the sheet from rust, oxidation and chemical attack.
  • Cost effective: Lower price than stainless steel with similar corrosion protection for most applications.
  • Good formability: Can be stamped, bent, deep drawn and fabricated without coating damage.
  • Weldable and paintable: Surface treatment (passivation, oiling) prepares it for painting or welding.
  • Flexible coating weight: From 40g/m² to 275g/m² to match the corrosion requirement of each application.

Applications

Galvanized steel plates are used in roofing and wall panels, air ducts, electrical cabinets, automotive parts, storage tanks, agricultural equipment, corrugated sheets and general fabrication.
