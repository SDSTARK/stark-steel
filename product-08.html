Galvanized steel pipe is a welded steel pipe with a galvanized or electro-galvanized layer on the surface. Galvanizing can increase the corrosion resistance of steel pipes and prolong their service life. Galvanized pipes are widely used. In addition to being used as pipeline pipes for general low-pressure fluids such as water, gas, and oil, they are also used as oil well pipes and oil pipelines in the petroleum industry, especially offshore oil fields, and oil heaters and condensation pipes for chemical coking equipment. Pipes for coolers, coal distillation washing oil exchangers, trestle piles, and support pipes for mine tunnels, etc.

Key Features

  • Reliable corrosion protection: Galvanized coating (40g – 275g/m²) extends service life in moisture-rich environments.
  • High strength and pressure resistance: Q195/Q235/Q345 steel grades support medium and high pressure applications.
  • Uniform zinc layer: Smooth, even coating with strong adhesion, no peeling during bending or cutting.
  • Complete size range: Seamless and welded round pipes from small to large diameters.
  • Threading and coupling: Available with plain ends, threaded ends or grooved ends per customer request.

Applications

Galvanized steel pipes are used for water supply, gas and oil pipelines, HVAC systems, scaffolding, greenhouses, agricultural irrigation, guardrails, fence posts and structural supports.
