Steel Wire Nails Description

Steel wire nails are one of the most widely used fasteners in construction, woodworking and packaging industries. Our nails are produced from high quality low carbon steel wire (Q195 / Q235) with uniform shank diameter, consistent hardness and sharp diamond points for smooth penetration into wood, concrete formwork and other materials.

We supply common wire nails, roofing nails, concrete nails, finishing nails and U-type nails in a wide range of diameters and lengths. Surface options include bright polished, electro-galvanized, hot-dip galvanized and color painted to suit indoor, outdoor and corrosion-resistant requirements.

Each batch is inspected for dimensions, tensile strength and surface finish. Nails are packed in bulk cartons, small boxes, plastic bags or on pallets for export shipment, ensuring they arrive at site ready for immediate use.

Key Features

  • Selected low carbon steel wire with good ductility and consistent mechanical properties.
  • Smooth shank, sharp diamond point and flat head for easy driving and firm holding.
  • Bright, electro-galvanized, hot-dip galvanized and painted finishes available.
  • Full range of sizes from 1.0 mm to 5.0 mm diameter and 20 mm to 120 mm length.
  • Clean surface, no oil stains, uniform coating thickness for galvanized types.
  • Custom packaging, private labeling and mixed container loading supported.

Applications

Steel wire nails are widely used in building construction, wooden formwork, furniture manufacturing, pallet and crate assembly, roofing felt fixing, drywall installation, interior decoration, packaging and general DIY projects. They are suitable for hand nailing, pneumatic nail guns and automated fastening equipment.
